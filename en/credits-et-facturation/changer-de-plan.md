# Change plan

## Principle

The subscription goes through **Shopify's native billing**: the plan applied in Yolysi is always that of the active Shopify subscription.

## Steps

1. Open **Billing**.
2. Under **Choose a plan**, find your plan (**Your plan** badge) and click **Get started →** on the one you want.
3. You're redirected to Shopify to **approve** the subscription.
4. Back in Yolysi, the message *… plan activated successfully* confirms the change and your points are reset according to the new plan.

## Cancel or downgrade

If the subscription is **cancelled, declined, expired or frozen** on Shopify's side, your account automatically goes back to the **Free** plan. A pending payment changes nothing until approved.

## What happens to your points

- **Monthly** points follow the new plan.
- **Packs** stay valid while the subscription is active ([details](comprendre-les-points.md)).
- Already-generated content is unaffected.
