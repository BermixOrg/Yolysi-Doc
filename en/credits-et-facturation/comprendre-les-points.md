# Understanding points

Yolysi runs on **points** (also called credits). Every action that produces or analyzes something has a fixed cost, shown **before** you launch it.

## Two kinds of points

| Kind | Lifetime |
|---|---|
| **Monthly** | Provided by your [plan](plans.md), **expire at the end of the period** (reset on renewal). |
| **Packs** | Bought on top, **stay valid as long as your subscription is active**. Availability and pricing: **TBD**. |

Your balance appears as *Monthly: X • Pack: Y* on the [dashboard](../demarrage/tableau-de-bord.md) and in **Billing**.

## Charging rules

- **Checked first**: if the balance is insufficient, the action is blocked with the message *Insufficient balance* (and the points required).
- **Charged only on success**: a failed generation costs nothing.
- **Free**: creating or editing a Brand Kit by hand, editing a draft, viewing history or monitoring.

## Transaction history

In **Billing → Transaction history**: date, description, amount and **balance after** for each charge or plan change.

## I'm out of points

Wait for the monthly renewal, [change plan](changer-de-plan.md) or, if available, add a pack.
