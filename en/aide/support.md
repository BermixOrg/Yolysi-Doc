# Contact support

## Open a ticket

1. In the app, open **Support**.
2. Click **New ticket**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Subject** *(required)*: your issue in a few words;
   - **Category**: General, Billing, Bug, Feature or Content;
   - **Priority**: Blocking bug, Non-blocking bug, Improvement or Info / Suggestion;
   - **Message** *(required)*: describe the issue in detail.
4. Click **Send**.

![New ticket window](../../images/aide/01-nouveau-ticket.png)

## Follow a ticket

The list shows your tickets, filterable by **Open**, **In progress**, **Resolved**, **Closed**. Select one to read the exchange with the team and reply (Enter to send). **Mark resolved** closes your request; a closed ticket can't receive messages: open a new one.

## Describing a problem well

- The page concerned and what you were trying to do.
- The **exact error message**.
- The approximate time and the type of content.
- What you already tried ([troubleshooting](depannage.md)).
