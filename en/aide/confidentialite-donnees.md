# Privacy and data

> Summary of Yolysi's privacy policy. If they differ, the policy published in the app prevails.

## What Yolysi processes

- Your **products'** titles and descriptions.
- The **generated content** (articles, pages, sheets).
- Your **Brand Kit**.
- *Only if you connect them*: audience and ranking data from **Google Search Console**, **Google Analytics 4** and **Google Ads**.

## What Yolysi does not request

No access to your store's **customers** or **orders**.

## Providers

| Provider | Role |
|---|---|
| OpenRouter | AI content generation (topic, keywords, catalog sent for writing) |
| DataForSEO | Keywords, trends, search results, backlinks |
| Supabase | Database and generated-image hosting |
| Vercel | Application hosting |
| Google | Search Console, Analytics 4, Ads (on your explicit connection) |

## AI-generated content

Always saved as a draft, to be reviewed before publishing. Yolysi never publishes automatically.

## Retention and deletion

Data is kept as long as the app is installed. On **uninstall**, Shopify notifies Yolysi, which deletes all of the store's data: content, configuration, access tokens and generated images. Exact timeframe: **TBD**.

## Contact

For any question or early deletion request: **yolysi.contact@gmail.com**.
