# Frequently asked questions

## Does Yolysi publish content on its own?
No, never. Everything is saved as a draft and you approve it. See the [lifecycle](../brouillons-et-publication/cycle-de-vie.md).

## Can I generate without a Brand Kit?
No: an active Brand Kit is required. [Creating one](../brand-kit/creer-son-brand-kit.md) takes a few minutes, or use [auto-fill](../brand-kit/auto-decouverte-ia.md).

## How much does a generation cost?
The cost is shown before each launch. Full list: [cost grid](../credits-et-facturation/grille-des-couts.md).

## Do I lose points if a generation fails?
No, points are only charged on success.

## Do my monthly points expire?
Yes, at the end of the period. Packs stay valid while the subscription is active. See [understanding points](../credits-et-facturation/comprendre-les-points.md).

## Can the AI be wrong?
Yes. Always review facts, prices and claims before publishing.

## What happens when I publish a product sheet?
The product's description is **replaced immediately**. See [publish to Shopify](../brouillons-et-publication/publier-sur-shopify.md).

## Can I write in a language other than the interface's?
Yes, content language is a separate setting. See [interface and content language](../parametres/langue-interface.md).

## Does Yolysi access my customers or orders?
No. See [privacy and data](confidentialite-donnees.md).

## Why is monitoring empty?
It needs published content and Google Search Console connected: [Connect Google](../monitoring/connecter-google.md).

## Are backlinks contacted automatically?
No. The audit is a decision aid, no message is sent.

## How do I contact support?
From the **Support** tab: see [Contact support](support.md).
