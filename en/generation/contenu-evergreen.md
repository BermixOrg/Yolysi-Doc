# Evergreen content

## What it's for

*Evergreen* content stays relevant years after publishing: complete guide, expert FAQ, glossary, pillar article. It builds your authority on a topic.

## Requirements

An [active Brand Kit](../brand-kit/creer-son-brand-kit.md). Enough balance: cost **TBD**.

## Steps

1. Choose **Evergreen content**.
2. Fill in:
   - **Authority topic**: the broad subject you want to be a reference for (e.g. "The complete guide to sustainable home decor").
   - **Angle / sub-topic** *(optional)*: narrows the targeted sub-topic (e.g. "Small spaces, tight budget").
   - **Content format**: Complete guide, Expert FAQ, Pillar article, Glossary, Text block or Custom format (describe it).
   - **Target length**: Short (800–1,200 words), Standard (1,500–2,500 words) or Long (3,000+ words).
   - **Output type**: **Text** or **Image**. *Video* and *Podcast* are marked "Coming soon".
3. Click **Generate content**.

## Publishing

Evergreen content is published as a **Shopify page** (unpublished). See [Publish to Shopify](../brouillons-et-publication/publier-sur-shopify.md).

## Common error

- **"Enter a topic to generate the content"**: the authority topic is required.
