# Optimized blog article (SERP analysis)

## What it's for

This is the most polished type. Before writing, Yolysi **analyzes Google results** for your keywords (competing articles, "People Also Ask" questions, featured snippets) and suggests **high-potential article angles**. Users' questions feed blocks designed for AI assistants' answers (AEO).

## Requirements

An [active Brand Kit](../brand-kit/creer-son-brand-kit.md) with pillar keywords filled in. Enough balance: cost **TBD**.

## Steps

1. On **Content production**, choose **Optimized blog article**.
2. Set up the form:
   - **Content language**: French, English, Spanish or German.
   - **SERP region**: which Google is analyzed (France, Belgium, Canada, Switzerland). Choose your store's main market.
   - **SEO foundation**: reminder of the Brand Kit keywords (**Edit** button).
3. Click **Run SERP analysis**. The AI analyzes Google results ("Semantic analysis in progress").
4. A window shows the **detected opportunities**: pick the angle you like.
5. Click **Generate this article**. Writing takes a moment ("Writing in progress…"), including internal linking and AEO blocks.
6. The success screen sends you to your **drafts**.

![Detected opportunities window](../../images/generation/02-opportunites-serp.png)

## Good to know

- The cost is charged **when generation succeeds**. The SERP analysis alone doesn't produce an article.
- The draft is saved automatically.
- If the analysis returns no relevant idea, broaden your pillar keywords in the Brand Kit.

## Common errors

- **"Select an article before generating"**: pick an opportunity in the window.
- **Insufficient balance**: see [understanding points](../credits-et-facturation/comprendre-les-points.md).
