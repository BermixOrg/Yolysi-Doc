# Product sheet

## What it's for

Replace a thin, supplier-copied or duplicated description with text centered on **benefits** and **conversion**, in your brand's tone.

## Requirements

An [active Brand Kit](../brand-kit/creer-son-brand-kit.md) and at least one product in your store. Enough balance: cost **TBD**.

## Steps

1. Choose **Product sheet**.
2. In **Choose a product**, search for the product (search bar); **Load more products** shows the rest of the catalog.
3. Select it. Generation starts at the cost shown.
4. Review the result in your [drafts](../brouillons-et-publication/relire-et-modifier.md).

## ⚠️ Before publishing

Unlike articles and pages, which are created unpublished, publishing a product sheet **directly replaces the product's description** in your store. Review carefully, and keep the old description if you want to be able to go back. Details: [Publish to Shopify](../brouillons-et-publication/publier-sur-shopify.md).

## Common error

- **"No product found in this store"**: check that the catalog contains products and that the app has access to products.
