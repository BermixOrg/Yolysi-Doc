# Article from a product

## What it's for

Promote a product in a **blog article** (uses, comparison, advice) rather than in a plain sheet. The product often serves as the call-to-action link.

## Requirements

An [active Brand Kit](../brand-kit/creer-son-brand-kit.md) and a product in the catalog. Enough balance: cost **TBD**.

## Steps

1. Choose **Article from a product**.
2. In **Choose the article's product**, search and select the product. The article will center on it.
3. Start the generation (cost shown).
4. Find the article in your drafts. When published, it's created like any [blog article](../brouillons-et-publication/publier-sur-shopify.md) (unpublished).

## Difference from the product sheet

The [product sheet](fiche-produit.md) rewrites the product's **description**; here you produce a **new article** and the product page is not modified.
