# Choose a content type

The **Content production** screen offers five tiles. The cost in points of each is shown on it (`… pts`) and repeated before launch.

![Content type selection screen](../../images/generation/01-choix-type.png)

| Type | What it produces | When to choose it | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Optimized blog article](article-optimise-serp.md) | An article written after analyzing Google's Top 10 | You want to rank on a specific query | TBD |
| [Article from an idea](article-via-idee.md) | A complete article from your idea or a news item | You already have a topic | TBD |
| [Article from a product](article-depuis-produit.md) | An article centered on a product from your catalog | You want to promote a product | TBD |
| [Product sheet](fiche-produit.md) | A product description focused on benefits and conversion | Your descriptions are thin or duplicated | TBD |
| [Evergreen content](contenu-evergreen.md) | Guides, glossaries, timeless pillar articles | You're building long-term authority | TBD |

All costs are detailed in the [cost grid](../credits-et-facturation/grille-des-couts.md).

## What's included in a blog article

At no extra cost: **images**, a call-to-action **product block** (in the style chosen in your Brand Kit) and **internal links** to your already-published content.

## Before you start

- An [active Brand Kit](../brand-kit/creer-son-brand-kit.md) is required.
- Your balance must cover the cost; otherwise the button shows *Insufficient balance*.
- Points are only charged if the generation succeeds.

> A banner reminds you: *AI-generated content — review before publishing.*

Writing tips: [Best practices](bonnes-pratiques.md).
