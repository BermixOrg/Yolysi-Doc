# Generation best practices

## Get the Brand Kit right first

The quality of everything generated depends on it: precise pillar keywords, a concrete persona, clear exclusion rules. An hour spent here improves every later generation. See [Create your Brand Kit](../brand-kit/creer-son-brand-kit.md).

## Write precise topics

| Instead of… | Write… |
|---|---|
| "Candles" | "How to choose a scented candle for a small room" |
| "Decor guide" | "Sustainable decor guide on a tight budget, using local materials" |

## Pick the right type

- A specific query to win → [optimized article](article-optimise-serp.md).
- A topic you already master → [article from an idea](article-via-idee.md).
- Lasting authority → [evergreen](contenu-evergreen.md).

## Respect the market

The **SERP region** and **language** should match your customers. A French article analyzed on Belgian Google won't face the same competitors as on French Google.

## Always review

The AI can get a price, a dimension or a product claim wrong. Check **figures**, **commercial promises** and **proper names** before publishing.

## Adjust, don't restart

Fix the draft directly, or use **Optimize with AI** on the content page ([review and edit](../brouillons-et-publication/relire-et-modifier.md)) rather than launching a full new generation.
