# Article from an idea

## What it's for

You already have a topic (a concept, a news item, a customer question): Yolysi develops it into a complete article, in your brand's tone.

## Requirements

An [active Brand Kit](../brand-kit/creer-son-brand-kit.md). Enough balance: cost **TBD**.

## Steps

1. Choose **Article from an idea**.
2. Fill in the form:
   - **Your idea or concept** (e.g. "Why solid oak outlasts pine for furniture longevity").
   - **Writing tone**: Inspiring & Creative, Factual & Technical, Friendly & Conversational, Expert & Authoritative, Luxury & Premium.
   - **Content language** and **SERP region**.
3. Click **Generate content**.
4. Find the result in your [drafts](../brouillons-et-publication/relire-et-modifier.md).

## Difference from the optimized article

No analysis of Google's Top 10: it's more direct, but the article isn't built from content that already ranks. To target a specific query, prefer the [optimized article](article-optimise-serp.md).

## Tip

The more precise the idea (angle, audience, constraint), the more useful the result. See [best practices](bonnes-pratiques.md).
