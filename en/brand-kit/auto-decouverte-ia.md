# AI auto-fill

## What it's for

Instead of typing everything, the AI analyzes your store and **suggests** a Brand Kit: tone, persona, slogan, pillar keywords and exclusion rules.

## How it works

The AI starts from your store's name and description and a sample of your product catalog. It does not cover colors: those are detected from the store's public theme.

## Steps

1. Open **Brand Kit**.
2. Click **Auto-fill with AI**. The button shows the cost in points.
3. Wait during the analysis ("Analyzing…").
4. The message *Brand profile suggested — review and save* confirms it's done.
5. **Review every field**, edit as needed, then click **Save**.

> Nothing is saved automatically: until you click **Save**, your Brand Kit is unchanged.

## Common errors

- **"Auto-discovery failed — try again"**: retry; if it keeps failing, create the Brand Kit by hand and contact [support](../aide/support.md).
- **Result too generic**: enrich your store description and product pages, then retry or complete by hand.
