# Multiple Brand Kits

## What it's for

If your store sells several product lines or brands with different identities, create one Brand Kit per identity and activate the one that matches the generation at hand.

## Where to manage them

In **Settings**, **Brand Kits** section: each Brand Kit is listed with its creation date, an **Active** badge for the one in use, and an edit button.

## Steps

- **Create**: **Create a new Brand Kit** button, then follow [Create your Brand Kit](creer-son-brand-kit.md).
- **Edit**: edit icon on the Brand Kit's row.
- **Switch Brand Kit when generating**: on the generation screen, the active Brand Kit is shown with an **Edit / Change** button.

## Good to know

- Only one Brand Kit is **active** at a time: it's the one applied to generation.
- Already-generated content doesn't change when you edit a Brand Kit; only future generations take it into account.
- Managing several *stores* within one installation isn't supported: one Brand Kit = one identity within a store.
