# Create your Brand Kit

## What it's for

The Brand Kit is the **foundation of every generation**: the AI applies it to each piece of content to stay true to your brand. You define it once, then refine it as your brand evolves.

## Requirements

None. It's actually the first thing to do: the **Content production** screen stays locked until an active Brand Kit exists.

## The four tabs

### Visual Identity
- **Brand name** and **slogan / tagline**.
- **Color palette**: detected automatically from your store's public theme. If a color can't be detected it stays empty rather than being invented. A real-time preview (call-to-action button) shows the result.

### Voice & Tone
- **Writing personality**: Expert & Authoritative, Friendly & Approachable, Luxury & Refined, Innovative & Bold.
- **Target persona**: describe your typical reader (e.g. "urban 30–45 year olds, environmentally conscious").
- **Product CTA style** in articles: Horizontal, Vertical card, Minimal banner or Recommendation badge. This is the product block automatically inserted at the end of an article.

### SEO & Market
- **Pillar keywords**: your priority topics. They steer the Google analysis behind generations.
- **Language**: language of the generated content (independent of the interface language, see [Interface language](../parametres/langue-interface.md)).
- **SERP country**: which Google is analyzed (for example France, Belgium, Canada, Switzerland). Choose your store's main market.

### Brand Safety
- **Exclusion rules**: topics or wording to never use (e.g. "Don't mention plastic"). Each active rule is listed and can be removed.

## Save

Click **Save**. Creating and manually editing the Brand Kit is **free**.

## Common errors

- **"No active Brand Kit"** when generating: create one, or activate one from [Multiple Brand Kits](plusieurs-brand-kits.md).
- **Content in the wrong language**: check the Brand Kit's *Language*, not the interface language.
