# Review and edit a draft

## Open a draft

In **Drafts**, filter by type (**All**, **Articles**, **Product sheets**, **Evergreen**), then:
- **Preview**: quick read in a window.
- **Open**: the content's full page.

![Drafts list](../../images/brouillons-et-publication/01-liste-brouillons.png)

## Edit

On the content page, click **Edit** to change:
- the **Title**;
- the **Content (HTML)**.

Click **Save** to confirm. Manual edits are free.

## Read the scores

The **Scores** card gives an SEO score and an AEO score. For a draft they're a reference point; see [how scores are calculated](../monitoring/score-aeo.md).

## Improve with AI

The **Optimize with AI** button creates a **new version** of the content without losing the previous one. A confirmation shows the cost in points (**TBD**, see the [cost grid](../credits-et-facturation/grille-des-couts.md)). If your balance is insufficient, the message shows the points required and available.

## Content information

The **Metadata** panel shows creation date, publication date and **version**; **Brand Kit** reminds you which one was used for generation; **Versions** lists the history.

## Before publishing: checklist

- [ ] Facts, prices and figures verified.
- [ ] Tone consistent with the brand.
- [ ] No topic excluded by your Brand Safety rules.
- [ ] Links and product blocks correct.
