# The content lifecycle

```
Generation (AI) → Draft → Review (you) → Shopify publication
```

## The stages

1. **Generation**: Yolysi produces the content from your [Brand Kit](../brand-kit/creer-son-brand-kit.md).
2. **Draft**: the content is saved and listed in **Drafts**. It doesn't exist in Shopify yet.
3. **Review**: you read, fix, and request an AI improvement if needed ([review and edit](relire-et-modifier.md)).
4. **Publication**: you send the content to Shopify ([publish](publier-sur-shopify.md)). Articles and pages arrive there **unpublished**; you keep a final validation in Shopify before going live.

## Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Draft** | Generated, not yet sent to Shopify. |
| **Published** | Sent to Shopify. It can then be [tracked in monitoring](../monitoring/vue-ensemble.md). |
| **Archived** | Set aside. |

## Versions

When you improve content with AI, a **new version** is created without losing previous ones. They're listed in the **Versions** section of the content page.

## Where to find your content

- **Drafts**: what's waiting for your approval.
- [**History**](historique.md): all content, all statuses.

> Yolysi **never** publishes on its own. No generation and no AI connection bypasses this step.
