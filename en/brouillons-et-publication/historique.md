# History

The **History** page lists **all** your generated content: drafts, published and archived.

![History page](../../images/brouillons-et-publication/02-historique.png)

## Filter and search

- **Status**: All, Drafts, Published, Archived.
- **Type**: All types, Articles, Products, Evergreen.
- **Search**: type a word from the title in *Search content…*.

Published content shows its publication date (*Published …*).

## Special cases

- *No content*: you haven't generated anything yet; start [your first generation](../generation/choisir-un-type.md).
- *No content matches these filters*: loosen the filters or the search.

## Drafts or History?

**Drafts** is for handling what's waiting for your approval; **History** is for finding everything.
