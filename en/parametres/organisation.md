# Organization and settings

The **Settings** page ("Organization & preferences") gathers the app's configuration.

## Organization

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| **Shopify store** | The store the app is linked to. |
| **Current plan** | Free, Starter, Pro or Enterprise ([details](../credits-et-facturation/plans.md)). |
| **Organization name** | Name displayed in the app. |
| **Interface language** | See [Interface language](langue-interface.md). |
| **Display currency** | Currency used for display. |

## Brand Kits

List of your Brand Kits, with creation and editing: see [Multiple Brand Kits](../brand-kit/plusieurs-brand-kits.md).

## AI connections

Generating the token for Claude, ChatGPT or another MCP client: see [Connect Claude or ChatGPT](../integrations-ia/connecter-claude-chatgpt.md).

## Quick links

Shortcuts to **Billing**, **Brand Kit**, **History**, **Monitoring** and **Support**.

## About

App name and version, and its operating status (*Operational*).
