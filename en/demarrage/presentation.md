# What Yolysi does

## What it's for

Yolysi helps Shopify merchants **produce and track content that ranks** on Google *and* in the answers of AI assistants (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude…). This is called **SEO** (classic search optimization) and **AEO** (*Answer Engine Optimization*). See the [glossary](../aide/glossaire.md).

## What you can do

- **Define your brand identity** once (the [Brand Kit](../brand-kit/creer-son-brand-kit.md)): tone, target audience, keywords, rules to follow.
- **Generate** blog articles, product sheets and evergreen content, in [five generation types](../generation/choisir-un-type.md).
- **Review, edit and publish** to Shopify, always after your approval ([lifecycle](../brouillons-et-publication/cycle-de-vie.md)).
- **Track results**: clicks, impressions, rankings, AI-sourced traffic, backlinks ([monitoring](../monitoring/vue-ensemble.md)).
- **Generate from your AI assistant** (Claude, ChatGPT…) through [AI connections](../integrations-ia/connecter-claude-chatgpt.md).

## How it works

Each generation uses your Brand Kit, real search data (trends, keyword volumes, Google results) and a language model. The result is saved as a **draft**. You pay in **points**; they are only charged if the generation succeeds ([details](../credits-et-facturation/comprendre-les-points.md)).

## What Yolysi does not do

- It **does not publish automatically**: everything goes through your approval.
- It **has no access to your customers or orders**.
- It **sends no link requests**: the backlink audit is a decision aid, not automated outreach.
- It does not (yet) offer an automatic editorial calendar, a built-in AI chat, or multi-store management inside the app.

> Content is produced by an AI: **always review it** before publishing (facts, prices, commercial claims).
