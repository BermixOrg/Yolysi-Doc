# The dashboard

The home page summarizes your account and takes you to the most common actions.

![Yolysi dashboard](../../images/demarrage/02-tableau-de-bord.png)

## Indicators

| Card | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Available credits** | Your balance, split into *Monthly* (expires) and *Pack* (persists). See [points](../credits-et-facturation/comprendre-les-points.md). |
| **Drafts** | Generated content awaiting review. |
| **Published** | Content sent to Shopify. |
| **Total content** | Drafts + published. |

## Active Brand Kit

Shows the Brand Kit used for generations and its pillar keywords. Buttons: **Edit Brand Kit**, or **Create a Brand Kit** if you don't have one yet.

## Quick actions

- **Drafts** — review and publish.
- **History** — all your generated content.
- **Credits & Billing** — manage your subscription.
- **Settings** — app configuration.

The **Generate content** button opens the generation screen directly.

## Monitoring preview

A table lists your tracked content with its score and 7-day change. Until Google Search Console is connected, a message invites you to do so: see [Connect Google](../monitoring/connecter-google.md).
