# Install the app

## Requirements

- A Shopify store.
- An account allowed to install apps on that store.

## Steps

1. Open Yolysi's page on the **Shopify App Store** and click **Add app**.
2. Shopify shows the permissions requested. Read them, then approve the installation.
3. Yolysi opens directly inside the Shopify admin. **No manual setup** is needed: your workspace is created automatically.
4. You land on the [dashboard](tableau-de-bord.md). Then continue with the [first steps](premiers-pas.md).

![Shopify permissions screen during installation](../../images/demarrage/01-installation-autorisations.png)

## Permissions requested

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Read products | Offer your products in generations and feed the Brand Kit. |
| Write products | Update a product's description when you publish a product sheet. |
| Read and write content | Create blog articles and pages. |
| Write files | Upload generated images to your store's Files. |

Yolysi **does not request any access to customers or orders**.
