# First steps in 5 stages

## Stage 1 — Create your Brand Kit

Nothing can be generated until a Brand Kit exists. Open **Brand Kit** and fill in your brand identity. You can save time with [AI auto-fill](../brand-kit/auto-decouverte-ia.md).
➡️ [Create your Brand Kit](../brand-kit/creer-son-brand-kit.md)

## Stage 2 — Generate a first piece of content

Go to **Content production**, pick a type, fill in the form and start the generation. The cost in points is shown before you launch it.
➡️ [Choose a content type](../generation/choisir-un-type.md)

## Stage 3 — Review the draft

The content appears in **Drafts**. Open it, review it, fix what needs fixing.
➡️ [Review and edit](../brouillons-et-publication/relire-et-modifier.md)

## Stage 4 — Publish to Shopify

Click **Publish to Shopify**. Articles and pages are created **unpublished** in Shopify: you keep a final check before going live.
➡️ [Publish to Shopify](../brouillons-et-publication/publier-sur-shopify.md)

> ⚠️ For a **product sheet**, the product's description is replaced directly. Read the page above before publishing.

## Stage 5 — Track the results

Connect Google Search Console (and optionally Analytics 4), then follow your published content.
➡️ [Connect Google](../monitoring/connecter-google.md)
