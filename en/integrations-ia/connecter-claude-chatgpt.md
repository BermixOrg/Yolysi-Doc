# Connect Claude or ChatGPT

## What it's for

Generate blog articles and evergreen content **from your AI assistant** (Claude, ChatGPT or any **MCP**-compatible client), without opening Yolysi. The content arrives as a **draft** in your dashboard, exactly as from the interface, with the same Brand Kit and the same points.

## Requirements

- Yolysi installed and an active [Brand Kit](../brand-kit/creer-son-brand-kit.md).
- An assistant or client that supports MCP servers.

## Steps

1. In Yolysi, open **Settings → AI connections**.
2. Click **Connect to Claude**.
3. Copy the **MCP server URL** and the **Access token**.
4. Paste them into your MCP client's configuration.

![AI connections section](../../images/integrations-ia/01-connexions-ia.png)

> ⚠️ **The token is only shown once.** Copy it right away. If you lose it, generate a new one.
>
> The token gives access to your account: don't share it.
