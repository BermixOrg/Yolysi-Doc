# Yolysi Documentation

Yolysi is a Shopify app that **generates, publishes and tracks SEO and AEO content** (optimized for search engines *and* for AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude), based on your brand identity.

> **Key principle:** Yolysi never publishes anything on its own. Every generated piece of content arrives as a **draft**, and you review it before sending it to your store.
