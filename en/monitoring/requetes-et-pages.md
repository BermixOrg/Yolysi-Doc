# Queries, pages and content

## Top Google queries

List of searches bringing you clicks: query, clicks, impressions, CTR, position. Useful to spot topics where you're gaining or slipping.

## Top performing pages

Ranking of your pages by results, with a dedicated page for **a specific page's queries**.

## Landing pages

Entry pages on the site with sessions and bounce rate, to see where visitors land. Requires [Analytics 4](connecter-google.md).

## A content's detail

From the **Blog**, **Evergreen** or **Products** tabs, open a piece of content to see:

- its **SEO** and **AEO** scores ([how they're calculated](score-aeo.md));
- the **analysis history** (date, scores, recommendations);
- the page's Search Console data.

### Link the page to Search Console

Enter the **page URL** (e.g. `https://www.mystore.com/blogs/news/my-article`) to match the content with its Search Console data. Without a URL: *Enter the URL above to see GSC data for this page.*

The **Analyze** button recalculates the content's scores. Cost: **TBD** (see [monitoring costs](tarif-monitoring.md)).
