# Backlinks `BETA`

> This feature is in **beta**.

## What it's for

A **backlink** is a link from another site to yours; they matter for SEO. This tab helps you understand your profile and spot sites that link to your competitors but not to you.

> **Nothing is sent automatically.** Yolysi contacts nobody: it's a visibility tool, you decide what to do with the list.

## Requirements

- Your site's domain: determined via [Search Console](connecter-google.md); without it, a message invites you to connect Search Console.
- Enough balance for an audit: cost **TBD**.

## Steps

1. **Monitoring → Backlinks**.
2. **Tracked competitors**: add up to **5** competitor domains, by hand (**Add**) or via **Suggest competitors** (allow 20 to 25 seconds; click a suggestion to add it). **Remove** deletes a domain.
3. Click **Run audit**.

![Backlinks page](../../images/monitoring/04-backlinks.png)

## Results

| Block | Content |
|---|---|
| **Backlink profile** | Analyzed domain, rank, backlinks, referring domains, broken backlinks, spam score. |
| **Domains to reach out to (competitor gap)** | Domains that link to a competitor but not to you. |
| **Priority prospects** | Among them, those linking to **at least two** of your competitors, ranked by authority. |

**Export to CSV** downloads the prospect list.

## Limits

- An audit can only be re-run **after a delay** (message *An audit already ran recently — try again later*).
- Your points balance is checked before the audit; it's only charged on success.
- If data is unavailable: *Backlinks unavailable right now*, try again later.

## History

Every audit is kept, so you can follow your profile's evolution over time.
