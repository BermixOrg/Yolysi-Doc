# Monitoring overview

Monitoring measures what happens to your **published content**: Google traffic, SEO/AEO quality, traffic coming from AI assistants.

## Requirements

- At least one [published](../brouillons-et-publication/publier-sur-shopify.md) piece of content (otherwise: *No published content*).
- [Google connected](connecter-google.md): Search Console required, Analytics 4 and Google Ads optional.

## The tabs

**Dashboard**, **Blog**, **Evergreen**, **Products**, **Backlinks**, **Setup**.

![Monitoring dashboard](../../images/monitoring/01-vue-ensemble.png)

## Period and comparison

Choose a duration (in days) or a **Custom** period (From / To). Tick **Compare to previous period** to see the change. The **Refresh** button reloads the data.

## Indicators

| Source | Indicators |
|---|---|
| Search Console | Clicks, impressions, **CTR** (share of people who click after seeing your link), average position |
| Analytics 4 | Sessions, engagement rate, bounce rate, new visitors, average session duration, page views, conversions |

Benchmarks (Good / Watch / Poor) compare your values with sector references, or with the previous period.

## Site health

The **Site health** card combines four pillars: **SEO**, **AEO**, **Web Vitals** and **Indexing**. If not all are measured yet, the score is partial (*Partial score — N pillar(s) measured*); run an analysis to complete it.

## Detailed sections

Clicks & position trend, top performing pages, top Google queries, tracked keywords, **AI traffic** (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude…), traffic sources, e-commerce funnel, organic vs paid, devices, geography, audience, customer journey.
