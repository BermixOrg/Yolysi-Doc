# Position tracking `BETA`

> This feature is in **beta**: it may change and some results are still being validated.

## What it's for

See where the **keywords your content actually targets** rank on Google, including those where you don't appear yet (**Not ranked**).

## Requirements

- A [published](../brouillons-et-publication/publier-sur-shopify.md) piece of content with keywords (those from the generation, otherwise the [Brand Kit](../brand-kit/creer-son-brand-kit.md) pillar keywords).
- The site's domain is determined from your [Search Console](connecter-google.md) property, otherwise from your store.

## Steps

1. Open **Monitoring** (dashboard or a content's detail).
2. In **Tracked keywords**, click **Refresh**.
3. Read the table: **Keyword**, **Position**, search **Volume**, change versus the previous reading.

![Tracked keywords table](../../images/monitoring/03-mots-cles-suivis.png)

## SERP features

For each keyword, Yolysi shows whether the results page contains an **AI Overview**, a **Featured snippet** or a **Local pack**.

## Limits

- Tracking is **on demand**, not continuous.
- Refresh is **limited to once every 6 hours** and to a capped number of keywords.
- Viewing the table never triggers a new measurement: only **Refresh** does.
- Every reading is kept: the progress history remains available.
