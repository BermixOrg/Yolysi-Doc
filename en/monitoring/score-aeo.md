# How SEO and AEO scores are calculated

Scores are **computed by fixed rules**, not by an AI: the same content with the same data always gets the same score. They range from 0 to 100.

## Reading them

| Score | Level |
|---|---|
| 70 and above | Good (green) |
| 40 to 69 | Average (orange) |
| Below 40 | Needs improvement (red) |

## SEO score

It takes into account, among others:
- **indexing** by Google;
- **average position** (the top 3 spots count the most);
- **CTR**;
- **Core Web Vitals** (real-world mobile performance);
- a consistent **canonical URL**;
- the presence of **rich results**.

## AEO score

It measures the content's ability to be picked up by answer engines:
- **non-indexed** content scores 0;
- structured answer blocks (AEO) and rich results raise the score.

## Site health

Five checks summarized in the health card: indexed by Google, consistent canonical, rich results detected, good Core Web Vitals, fresh content (under 6 months).

## Good to know

- A high score **does not guarantee** a good ranking: it's a best-practice indicator.
- Very recent content may score low for lack of data: give it time.
- The exact weightings may change; the criteria above remain the reference.
